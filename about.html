Meet CH

Six years. One studio. One belief.

Chandu S R Krishna founded ch+partners in 2018 as a practice in architecture, structural engineering, sustainability and interiors — a single studio built to carry a project from the first sketch to the last light fixture, without handing it off between disciplines along the way.

"We believe that by working together, we can create a better future for all."

Chandu S R Krishna, Founder
How the studio works

People first, discipline second.

The practice describes itself as a single entity, ethnically and culturally diverse, with people kept at the center of every project — not a hierarchy of specialists who meet only at handoff.

A friendly and collaborative atmosphere, where every idea on the team is valued and respected — the same posture the studio brings to a client's own opinions about their home.

Six years of experience optimizing what a client actually spends against what they actually get — sustainable, innovative solutions that serve the household and the environment at once.
